Privacy

Frame Heart has no accounts, no analytics and no ads. Everything runs on your computer. It only goes online when you ask it to download a model or FFmpeg.

On your computer

Your pictures, video and settings stay on your computer. Frame Heart reads the files you give it and writes cleaned copies to the folder you choose. Nothing is uploaded.

When it goes online

Only when you ask. Settings can download FFmpeg, and the model list can download upscaling models. Those downloads come from the projects that publish them, which see an ordinary download request.

This website

It’s hosted by GitHub Pages and uses Google Fonts. It sets no cookies of its own. Both services see an ordinary web request.
